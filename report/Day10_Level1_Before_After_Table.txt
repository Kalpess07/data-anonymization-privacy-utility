2. Transformation Summary

- zip_code: mask the final two characters of valid five-digit values, such as 93810 becoming 938**.

3. Interpretation

Generalization reduces the precision of age values, while masking hides part of the ZIP-like value. These transformations reduce detail but do not, by themselves, prove that the dataset satisfies k-anonymity or guarantee protection against re-identification.
