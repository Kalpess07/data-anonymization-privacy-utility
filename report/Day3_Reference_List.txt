REFERENCE LIST
Project: Empirical Research Investigation on Data Anonymization Techniques:
Evaluating the Privacy-Utility Trade-off in ML Models

Purpose
This document organizes foundational references for the team's literature review on k-anonymity, re-identification risk, and the Netflix Prize case. Verify access to each publication and check the final citation details before submitting the report.

1. K-ANONYMITY AND PRIVACY MODELS

Sweeney, L. (2002). k-Anonymity: A Model for Protecting Privacy.
International Journal of Uncertainty, Fuzziness and Knowledge-Based Systems, 10(5), 557-570.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218488502001648
Relevance: Introduces k-anonymity as a privacy model based on making each record indistinguishable from at least k-1 other records with respect to selected quasi-identifiers.

2. RE-IDENTIFICATION RISK

Narayanan, A., & Shmatikov, V. (2008). Robust De-anonymization of Large Sparse Datasets.
Proceedings of the 2008 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy, 111-125.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/SP.2008.33
Relevance: Studies de-anonymization of sparse, high-dimensional data and demonstrates how auxiliary information can help link records across datasets.

3. NETFLIX PRIZE RE-IDENTIFICATION CASE

Narayanan, A., & Shmatikov, V. (2008). Robust De-anonymization of Large Sparse Datasets.
Proceedings of the 2008 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy, 111-125.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/SP.2008.33
Relevance: This paper examines the Netflix Prize dataset and shows how apparently anonymized movie-rating records could be linked with external information. It is directly relevant to the project's discussion of re-identification risk.

4. PRIVACY AND DATA UTILITY

Sweeney, L. (2002). k-Anonymity: A Model for Protecting Privacy.
International Journal of Uncertainty, Fuzziness and Knowledge-Based Systems, 10(5), 557-570.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218488502001648
Relevance: Useful for explaining how generalization and suppression can reduce disclosure risk, while also motivating the need to consider the usefulness of transformed data.
